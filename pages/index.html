FAQs

Is it safe to get TRT or peptide treatment?

If a doctor is watching over you, hormone replacement therapy is safe. Our board-certified professionals closely monitor your therapy and adjust it based on lab results to ensure it is safe and yields optimal results. All FDA-approved drugs are available at certified US pharmacies. For long-term maintenance, periodic testing every 6-12 months helps us monitor how your metabolism adapts over time and allows us to make evidence-based adjustments to your nutrition and exercise plans.
